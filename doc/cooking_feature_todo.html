Cooking feature: wishlist / TODO

TouhouTB PvE camp cooking. Recap of what's agreed, what to build, and what's still open.

Agreed design

  • Cooking is a camp feature in PvE raids, and it's instant with no timers.
  • Deterministic in v1: recipe + ingredients gives a fixed meal.
  • Assign one or more characters to cook; the cook gets a bonus from the meal they made.
  • Recipes are per character. Only a character who learned a recipe can cook it, so who you bring on a raid matters.
  • Meals are pre-battle buffs; duration and target vary per meal.
  • Packed meals: pre-cooked meals can be brought into a raid at a flat potency penalty.
  • Carry weight limits what you bring. Mid-raid, over the limit means choosing what loot to drop, ingredients included.
  • Ingredients come from PvE raids: server-authoritative, solo first.
  • The PvE server is its own module; offline mode uses separate data.
  • Meals are PvE-only.
  • Economy is an intended pillar; trading comes later.
