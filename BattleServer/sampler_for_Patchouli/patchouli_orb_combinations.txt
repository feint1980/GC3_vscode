PATCHOULI ORB COMBINATIONS
5 orb slots, elements: Metal, Wood, Earth, Water, Fire

Rule recap:
  Metal kills Wood, Wood kills Earth, Earth kills Water, Water kills Fire, Fire kills Metal.
  Placing the VICTIM while its killer is stacked >=2 trims the killer to 1 (rescue).
  Placing the KILLER, reaching >=2, while its victim exists wipes all copies of the victim (attack).
  As a result, only certain 5-orb combinations are stable/reachable.

Total valid combinations: 56 (out of 126 unrestricted)

=== 5-of-a-kind (5 combos) ===
Metal x5 | Silver Meridian |
Summon a javelin that goes throughts the rows, dealling dmg 100%/50%/25% to all the character that stand in the way 


Wood x5 | Verdant Prison | 
Summons bramble that root enemy on contact ( last 1 turn ) on opponent field (including the free and occupied cells)


Earth x5 | Gaia's Bulwark | 
Can either target opponent or your feild, summon a stonewall that immoble on all the free cells, each wall has high HP 

Water x5 | Tidal Reckoning | 
Cast forward or backward → determines push vs. pull
All characters hit by the wave get displaced ±1 cell in that direction
Everyone displaced also gets inflicted with Fall Down status


Fire x5 | Royal Flare | 
Deal massive damage to everything in the field except Patchouli
