[Scene: Hakugyokurou — night. The garden is quiet, the moon pale. Cherry petals drift through the still air.]

Youmu: folds the newspaper, frowning “So they’re saying the whole incident was the ferryman’s fault. Sleeping on duty…”
She sighs. “Honestly, that shinigami causes more trouble than she solves.”

Yuyuko: sitting under a cherry tree, voice soft, almost humming “Ara, ara~ How cruel, Youmu. Her work is endless — it’s only natural she’d drift off once in a while.”

Youmu: turns slightly, skeptical “You’re defending her now?”

Yuyuko: smiles faintly, eyes distant “Mmm… not defending. Just remembering. Even the living forget how heavy the dead can be.”

Youmu looks puzzled. A single petal lands on her hand — she watches it dissolve into mist.

Youmu: “...Then this incident wasn’t her fault?”

Yuyuko: eyes half-closed, voice quiet “Fault, reason, blame… they’re all the same weight, in the end. And some weights aren’t meant to be lifted.”

Youmu opens her mouth to reply, but Yuyuko’s gaze has already drifted beyond her — toward the far horizon, where the boundary meets the void.

Yuyuko: “…Let’s just say, the river flowed where it had to.”

The wind stirs. Cherry petals rise into the night. Youmu stands silently as the screen fades to white.
